# GitHub release text – Sailfeed 1.1.0

Tag `v1.1.0`, title "Sailfeed 1.1.0". Paste everything below the line into the release description and attach the RPMs (aarch64, armv7hl, i486).

---

### New
- **Subscribe to website** – enter or paste the address of a website, an article or a feed. Sailfeed finds its feeds (announced in the page, linked, in parent pages or at the usual addresses of common blog systems), checks them and shows each with its newest articles. Feeds you already subscribe to are recognised; comment feeds and podcasts are marked. Also from the pulley of the web view.
- **Search** in the articles on your device – titles, or titles and text, in all feeds or the current selection, read ones included – and in feed titles ("Search" in the pulley of the article list and the feed list).
- **Links in articles** open in reader mode inside the app or in the browser (setting); a long press opens them the other way. Each link opens as its own page with a back button.
- **Easier first start** – the sign-in page explains Nextcloud News (with links), empty lists say how to subscribe, and short tips show the hidden gestures, one at a time (press and hold the icon in About to see them again).
- New app icon.

### Reading
- The bottom bar switches between reader mode and the original website; share, copy link, open in browser and subscribe are in a shorter pulley.
- Article preview: a calm "Show full article" row instead of two buttons; no more large gap below the title of articles that start with an image.
- Reader mode appears without the original page flashing up; previous/next slides in from the matching side.
- The bottom bar is easy to read in light ambiences.

### Article list
- Room below the camera when paging, a shorter swipe turns the page, no highlight when a swipe starts.
- "Show read articles" / "Load older articles" always in the same place; articles at the end of the list are marked as read reliably.

### Settings
- A short main page with a summary per subject; the settings on sub-pages: article list, article preview, reader mode, Nextcloud (account and backup), privacy.
- Live preview of the reader mode that stays in view while you change font, colors, spacing and margins; mark-as-read delay as a choice.
- Fix: some choices could change by themselves when the settings page opened.

### Other
- English texts without "(s)" plurals; German texts consistently use "du" and "Webseite".

### Install
Through OpenRepos / Storeman, or download the RPM for your device below (aarch64 for current devices such as the Jolla Phone (2026), armv7hl for older 32-bit devices, i486 for the emulator and Intel tablets). Sailfeed needs a Nextcloud server with the **News** app.
